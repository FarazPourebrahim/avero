## Durations

Components use Tailwind's duration utilities: `duration-150` is the default, `duration-200` the most
common, `duration-300` drawers and accordions, and `duration-500` image zooms.

## Reduced motion

`@averoui/tokens/base.css` shortens animations and transitions to near zero under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Import it, or apply the same rule in your own base styles.
