## Usage

```tsx
<ReactionBar
  likes={course.likes}
  liked={course.likedByMe}
  onLike={toggleLike}
  views={course.views}
  capacity="حداکثر ۱۵ نفر"
  saved={course.savedByMe}
  onSave={toggleSave}
/>
```

Each pill appears only when it has a value, so an item without a capacity limit simply has three
pills. Anything extra goes in as children, at the end of the row.

## Counts

`likes` and `views` are printed exactly as given, so the digits are your choice. Format them with
`useAveroFormatter` when you want the locale's digits.

## Toggles

Both toggles are buttons with `aria-pressed` rather than checkboxes, and the attribute follows
`liked` and `saved`, so a screen reader learns whether the viewer has already liked or saved the
item.

## Do and don't

- **Do** update `liked` and `likes` together — an optimistic update that moves one without the
  other reads as a bug.
- **Don't** use the bar for navigation; every pill here is a state change or a count.
