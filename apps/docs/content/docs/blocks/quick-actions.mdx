## Usage

```tsx
<QuickActions label="دسترسی سریع">
  <ActionTile icon={<PlusIcon />} onClick={addCourse}>
    افزودن دوره
  </ActionTile>
  <ActionTile icon={<CalendarPlusIcon />} tone="purple" onClick={addSession}>
    افزودن جلسه
  </ActionTile>
</QuickActions>
```

The tiles are children: `ActionTile` is its own component, and a grid that only accepted a fixed
shape would rule out a link tile or a disabled one.

## Columns

Every row starts at two columns on a phone and three on a tablet; `columns` sets the count from
`lg` up — 4, 5 or 6.

## Accessibility

Without a label the grid is a bare `div`, so its buttons are announced one after another with
nothing tying them together. Passing `label` makes it a named group — worth doing whenever the
tiles belong to one heading that is not directly above them.

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep the labels to two or three words — they sit under the icon and wrap tightly.
- **Don't** mix destructive actions in; the tiles all look equally inviting.
