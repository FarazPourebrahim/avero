## Composition

Built from `CapacityMeter`, `Chip` and `DisabledOverlay`. The capacity block is entirely
`CapacityMeter variant="card"` — the pill turns red on its own once the meter is full:

```tsx
<OpportunityCard
  capacityLabel="ظرفیت ارسال رزومه"
  value={3}
  max={12}
  status="9 جای خالی"
  startCaption="3 رزومه ارسال شده"
  endCaption="حداکثر 12 نفر"
/>
```

Carousel sizing (`w-[85%]`, `snap-center`, `flex-none`) belongs to the carousel rather than the
card, so pass it through `className`.

## The capacity-full state

`full` reproduces the reference's closed card: the surface dims and blurs, pointer events stop, the
call to action disappears, and `DisabledOverlay` covers it with a reason pill.

The reference's overlay also carries `bg-bg-[var(--bg-card)]/40` — a typo that never compiles
(defect R-06) — so only the backdrop blur actually renders. Avero reproduces the rendered result.

## Accessibility

- The closed card sets `aria-disabled` alongside `pointer-events-none`, so assistive technology is
  told what the styling implies rather than only being unable to click it.
- The `→` in the call to action is decorative and hidden from assistive technology; the label itself
  comes from the `view` dictionary entry.

## Do and don't

- **Do** pass `status` text that matches the remaining places; the pill colour follows the meter.
- **Don't** rely on `full` alone to prevent navigation server-side — it is a presentation state.
