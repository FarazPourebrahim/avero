## Anatomy

| Part     | Slot                                 | Notes                                      |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Root     | `data-slot="opportunity-card"`       | One `<a>`; `data-full` marks a closed card |
| Title    | `data-slot="opportunity-card-title"` | Clamped to two lines                       |
| Tags     | `data-slot="opportunity-card-tags"`  | Short tag chips                            |
| Capacity | `CapacityMeter variant="card"`       | Pill, bar and the two captions             |

## Composition

Built from `CapacityMeter`, `Chip` and `DisabledOverlay`. The capacity block is entirely
`CapacityMeter variant="card"` — the pill turns red on its own once the meter is full:

```tsx
<OpportunityCard
  capacityLabel="ظرفیت ثبت‌نام"
  value={3}
  max={12}
  status="9 جای خالی"
  startCaption="3 نفر ثبت‌نام کرده‌اند"
  endCaption="حداکثر 12 نفر"
/>
```

Carousel sizing (`w-[85%]`, `snap-center`, `flex-none`) belongs to the carousel rather than the
card, so pass it through `className`.

## The capacity-full state

`full` closes the card: the surface dims and blurs, pointer events stop, the call to action
disappears, and `DisabledOverlay` covers it with a reason pill. The overlay renders a backdrop blur
only, so the dimmed card stays visible behind the reason.

## Accessibility

- The closed card sets `aria-disabled` alongside `pointer-events-none`, so assistive technology is
  told what the styling implies rather than only being unable to click it.
- The `→` in the call to action is decorative and hidden from assistive technology; the label itself
  comes from the dictionary.

## Do and don't

- **Do** pass `status` text that matches the remaining places; the pill colour follows the meter.
- **Don't** rely on `full` alone to prevent sign-ups server-side — it is a presentation state.
