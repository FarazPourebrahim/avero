## Anatomy

| Part          | Slot                         | Notes                                                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| SectionHeader | `data-slot="section-header"` | Heading (`h2` by default) with optional subtitle, icon and actions |
| GlowOrbs      | `data-slot="glow-orbs"`      | Two blurred decorative circles                                     |

## Right-to-left

The accent bar, dot and icon sit at the inline start; actions at the inline end. The orbs sit at the logical corners.

## Composition

- Pass carousel controls or counts through `actions`.
- Put `GlowOrbs` inside a `relative overflow-hidden` container and lift the content with `relative z-10`.

## Accessibility

- Use `as` to keep heading levels in order. The bar, dot and orbs are decorative.

## Do and don't

- **Do** use `accentBar` once per page, for the page title.
