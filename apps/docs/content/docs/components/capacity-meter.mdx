| Bar    | `data-slot="progress"`              | [Progress](./progress)                    |

## Right-to-left

The bar fills from the inline start, and the captions sit at the start and end of the bar.

## Composition

- `detail` is the full meter for a detail page; `card` is the compact one for cards in a list.
- The status tone is derived from `value >= max`, so `status` only needs the wording.

## Accessibility

- The bar is labelled by the visible label, so it is never an unnamed `progressbar`.
- `data-state="full"` lets you style or announce a full meter without re-deriving the maths.

## Do and don't

- **Do** show both numbers; a bar alone is imprecise.
- **Don't** exceed `max` to fake a full bar — the value is clamped anyway.
