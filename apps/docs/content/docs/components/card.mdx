## Anatomy

| Part       | Slot                      | Notes                                                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Card       | `data-slot="card"`        | A `<div>`, or your element with `asChild`                                       |
| CardHeader | `data-slot="card-header"` | Title at the start, meta or actions at the end                                  |
| CardTitle  | `data-slot="card-title"`  | An `h3` by default; an icon before the text is sized and tinted as a title icon |
| CardFooter | `data-slot="card-footer"` | A row separated by a top border                                                 |

## Examples

### Surfaces

`surface` is the white `rounded-3xl` content card and `flat` the dashboard's `rounded-2xl` one.
`glass` is the translucent listing card that turns white on hover, and `muted` the slate tile.

<ComponentPreview name="card/variants" />

### Elevation

The shadow scale runs from the hairline `xs` to the soft `ambient` and brand-tinted `brand`. Pick
one tier per surface level and keep it consistent across a page.

<ComponentPreview name="card/elevation" />

### Clickable cards

`interactive` lifts the shadow on hover. Pair it with `asChild` so the whole card is one real link
rather than a div with a click handler.

<ComponentPreview name="card/interactive" />

## Right-to-left

Cards have no direction-specific styling; headers and footers mirror automatically.

## Composition

Cards are containers, so the surfaces are meant to nest: a `surface` content card holding `flat` or
`muted` sub-cards is the dashboard's usual shape.

<ComponentPreview name="card/surfaces" />

- Use `asChild` to render an `<article>` or make the whole card a link.
- `interactive` adds the hover shadow used by clickable cards.

## Accessibility

- Choose `CardTitle`'s `as` so heading levels follow the page outline.
- When the whole card is a link, keep a single link and don't nest other interactive elements.

## Theming

| Token                                                                                       | Used by     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| `--color-surface-glass`                                                                     | `glass`     |
| `--shadow-card-soft`, `--shadow-card-ambient`, `--shadow-card-faint`, `--shadow-brand-soft` | `elevation` |

## Do and don't

- **Do** keep one visual style per list of cards.
- **Don't** stack shadows and borders beyond what a variant provides.
