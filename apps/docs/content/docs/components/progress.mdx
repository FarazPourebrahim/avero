## Right-to-left

The fill grows from the inline start: right to left in RTL, left to right in LTR.

## Composition

- Pair it with a label row above, as in the capacity example; `CapacityMeter` (D-16) composes this for you.

## Accessibility

- Exposes `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow` and `aria-valuemax`; `value={null}` is indeterminate.
- Always give it an accessible name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
