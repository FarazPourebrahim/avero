## Anatomy

| Part           | Slot                                | Notes                                  |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| RadioGroup     | `data-slot="radio-group"`           | `role="radiogroup"`, a grid of options |
| RadioGroupItem | `data-slot="radio-group-item"`      | `role="radio"`, a round button         |
| Indicator      | `data-slot="radio-group-indicator"` | The blue dot of the chosen option      |

An item is 24px with a 2px outline, on the same soft palette as [Checkbox](./checkbox): a light
gray outline while unselected, and a blue tint with a blue dot once chosen. The dot pops in when an
option is chosen, and pressing shrinks the item slightly, like a [Button](./button).

`RadioCard` is an item presented as a card:

| Part        | Slot                                 | Notes                                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Card        | `data-slot="radio-card"`             | A `<label>`: the whole card chooses the option      |
| Title       | `data-slot="radio-card-title"`       | Names the radio through `aria-labelledby`           |
| Description | `data-slot="radio-card-description"` | Describes the radio through `aria-describedby`      |
| Aside       | `data-slot="radio-card-aside"`       | Optional content at the inline end, such as a price |

## Examples

### Validation

A radio group is labelled by its own heading with `aria-labelledby`, not by a `<label>`, because a
group is not a labelable element. Wire the message with `aria-describedby` and mark the group
`aria-invalid` while it is unanswered.

<ComponentPreview name="radio-group/validation" />

While the group is `aria-invalid`, every option's outline turns red, and so does its focus ring.

### Card options

`RadioCard` presents each option as a bordered card with a title, a line of explanation and an
optional aside. The whole card is the click target, and the chosen card takes a soft blue tint. Use
it where each choice needs explaining — shipping, plans, payment methods.

## Right-to-left

`RadioGroup` reads its direction from `AveroProvider`, so in Persian <kbd>←</kbd> moves to the next
option and in English <kbd>→</kbd> does. Pass `dir` to override it for one group.

## Composition

- Name the group: give it `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible heading, or `aria-label`.
- Give each item its own `<label htmlFor>`; clicking the label selects the option.
- `RadioCard` goes inside a `RadioGroup` in place of `RadioGroupItem`, takes every item prop, and
  forwards its ref to the radio, so arrow keys, `value` and form submission are unchanged.
  `className` styles the card; `radioClassName` styles the radio.
- Inside a `<form>`, the chosen `value` is submitted under the group's `name`.

## Accessibility

| Key                                                 | Action                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd>                                      | Moves focus to the chosen option, or the first one |
| <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Moves to and selects the next or previous option   |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>                                    | Selects the focused option                         |

- The group is a single tab stop; disabled options are skipped.
- A card's title is the radio's accessible name and its description the accessible description.
- The dot's pop settles at once under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Contrast trade-off.** The unselected outline is `gray-300`: 1.47:1 on white and 1.34:1 on the
  page background, below the 3:1 WCAG asks of a control's boundary — the same deliberate choice as
  [Checkbox](./checkbox), recorded in `docs/known-debts.md`. Where strict AA matters, pass
  `className="border-gray-500/80"` to each `RadioGroupItem`. The chosen state passes: the dot is
  4.82:1 on its tinted fill.

## API reference

`RadioGroup` and `RadioGroupItem` accept the
[Radix Radio Group](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/radio-group) props unchanged:
`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `name`, `required`, `disabled`, `orientation` and `dir` on
the group, and `value` and `disabled` on each item.

## Do and don't

- **Do** use a radio group for two to about six mutually exclusive options.
- **Don't** leave a group without a default when one answer is clearly the common one.
