Add `Combobox`, a searchable single select on Radix Popover. Options can be grouped under category headings, and matching ignores the differences between Arabic and Persian letters, zero-width non-joiners and digit systems. It follows the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern, works with `Field`, and submits its value through `name`. Also adds the `comboboxEmpty` dictionary entry and the `normalizeSearchText` helper.
