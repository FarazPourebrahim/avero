Add `DatePicker`, a Solar Hijri–first date field with `single` and `range` modes. Values are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings; dates can be typed year first in Persian, Arabic or Latin digits, or picked from a calendar that follows the WAI-ARIA date picker dialog pattern with direction-aware arrow keys. It supports `min`, `max` and `isDateDisabled`, follows the `AveroProvider` calendar and digits, and converts between calendars with `Intl` only. Also adds `CalendarIcon` and the `datePicker*` dictionary entries.
