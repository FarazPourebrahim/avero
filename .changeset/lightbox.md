Add `Lightbox`, a full-screen image viewer over a dark scrim, built on Radix Dialog. It pages through `images` with previous and next buttons, arrow keys that follow the reading direction, and `Home`/`End`; shows a counter in the locale's digits and an optional caption; and zooms the image from a toggle button or a click. Focus is trapped while it is open and returns to what opened it. The open state and the index can each be controlled. Also adds the `lightboxLabel` dictionary entry.
